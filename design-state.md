# Design State: The Gatefold Studio

## Brief

- **Problem:** Website uses placeholder content and an outdated 4-service structure.
- **Primary persona:** Senior decision-makers / Ops leaders valuing directness.
- **Success metric:** Seamless transition to 5-microsite routing + active /care, /discovery, /partner pages.
- **Brief document:** [docs/designpowers/briefs/2026-05-11-website-content-overhaul.md](docs/designpowers/briefs/2026-05-11-website-content-overhaul.md)

## Personas

- [Inclusive Personas](docs/designpowers/personas/2026-05-09-gatefold-studio-personas.md)
- Senior Dev / Creative Director — Values craft, depth, and "no-theatre" communication.

## Design Principles

1. **Physicality:** Everything feels like paper, vinyl, or a gatefold sleeve.
2. **High-Fidelity:** Stunning high-res visuals and world-class typography.
3. **Narrative-First:** Content is told as a "novel," not a bulleted list.
4. **Intentionality:** Every interaction (unfolding) is a reward for the user.
5. **Directness:** Blunt, senior-only communication. No agency theatre.

## Taste Profile

- **Emotional target:** Calm confidence, grave intention.
- **Quality level:** Flagship
- **Key references:** Vinyl record liner notes, high-end editorial design.
- **Aesthetic principles:** Distinctive, Contextual, No AI slop, No corporate fog.
- **Taste document:** [CLAUDE.md#taste-standards](CLAUDE.md#taste-standards)
