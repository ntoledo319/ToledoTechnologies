Daedalus Landed

A nonprofit initiative we're building.

We're building free (or radically affordable) AI systems that actually help people and don't treat them as ad inventory, data exhaust, or whales to be monetized.

Mission: Take the profit motive out of the basics of being human—starting with mental health, creation, and education.

View on GitHub → Get Involved

Why This Exists

Right now, to get serious AI for your life or work, you mostly have to:

  • ✗ Pay for premium models every month
  • ✗ Hand over your data, behavior, and attention
  • ✗ Or settle for weak "free" tools that quietly exploit you

Daedalus Landed is trying a different path: Build strong, humane AI systems under a nonprofit umbrella, keep the core tools free or radically cheap, and refuse to fund it by selling users out.

Early-stage and evolving. The mission is stable.

Flagship Projects

🌒

NOUS

"Not Your Therapist" — But Actually Helpful

A mental-health-aware AI companion and next-gen personal assistant. NOUS helps users practice real therapeutic tools (CBT, DBT), track patterns over time, and build coping plans—all with clear limits and safety rails.

What It Does

  • → Real skills engine, not just "positive vibes"
  • → Pattern tracking and plain-language reflection
  • → Calendar, tasks, and habit integration
  • → Hard edges around what it won't do
  • → Crisis escalation to human professionals

What It's Not

  • ✗ Not a licensed clinician
  • ✗ Not a crisis service
  • ✗ Not a replacement for real therapy
Early pre-beta
🛠

ENODIOS

The Everything Agent for People Who Can't Afford $20/Tool

AI is becoming "paywall your way to power or get left behind." ENODIOS takes free and low-cost models, orchestrates them smartly, and squeezes every ounce of value so normal people can build anything.

What It Does

  • → Code generation and refactoring
  • → Content, copy, and research
  • → Creative work: ideas, outlines, drafts
  • → Multi-model, open-first stack
  • → Reusable templates and pipelines
Pre-beta, expanding from coding to general creation

Values & Ethics

People > Product

Users are humans, not "MAUs" to optimize. We build for people, not metrics.

Open > Opaque

Code, decisions, and failures should be explainable. Transparency by default.

Non-Extractive

No dark patterns, no casino UX, no secret data resale. Ever.

Safety First

Especially around mental health, crisis content, and vulnerable users.

Environmentally Conscious

Model choice, caching, and infra design factor in energy cost, not just latency.

Free or Radically Affordable

The basics of being human shouldn't be paywalled.

Contributing

We're looking for:

  • → Engineers — Python, TypeScript, orchestration, infra, security
  • → UX/Product — Flows that don't treat people like stats
  • → Clinicians/Researchers — To design and evaluate NOUS responsibly

Early-stage work with room to shape direction. If you like building something meaningful, reach out.

Mental Health Disclaimer

NOUS and any related tools do not replace professional mental health care, do not provide crisis intervention, and should never be your only support in a life-threatening situation.

If you are in immediate danger or crisis, contact your local emergency number or a trusted crisis hotline in your region.
