SiteLift Fit Check ($750 fixed)

The smallest paid front door. Productized triage for productized WordPress exits. We confirm which of the four SiteLift packages fits, which destination platform makes sense, and any data risks before quoting the full project. One to two business days.

  • For: Small businesses on WordPress, Squarespace, or Wix
  • Inquiry: sitelift.toledotechnologies.com/fit-check →

Website Migration Audit ($1,500 fixed)

The deeper migration discovery. For 25+ page sites with real SEO equity, custom design ambition, or compliance needs. Three to five business days.

  • For: Established sites where the rebuild quote depends on what we find inside
  • Inquiry: web.toledotechnologies.com/audit →

Web App Discovery ($3,500 – $5,000)

Required first step for any internal tool, customer portal, dashboard, or custom web app. Maps the feature set, the database schema, the integrations, and the user roles. One to two weeks.

  • For: Operations leaders, ops-heavy businesses
  • Inquiry: apps.toledotechnologies.com/discovery →

AI Automation Blueprint ($3,500 – $10,000)

Required first step for any production AI engagement. Maps the data sources, the autonomy level, the governance posture, the integration surface. One to three weeks.

  • For: Anyone building AI that touches business data or autonomous action
  • Inquiry: ai.toledotechnologies.com/discovery →

Mobile App Discovery ($5,000 – $7,500)

Required first step for any iOS, Android, or cross-platform engagement. Maps the screen set, the hardware features, the App Store risks, and the launch plan. One to two weeks.

  • For: Product owners, consumer brands
  • Inquiry: mobile.toledotechnologies.com/discovery →

SaaS / Product Strategy ($7,500 – $15,000)

  • For: SaaS founders at idea, post-validation, or repositioning
  • Inquiry: apps.toledotechnologies.com/discovery (SaaS track) →

Why every project starts here

A free consultation is paid for in someone else's time, and that someone is usually the person doing the work. We don't run free consultations because the people who would do the discovery work aren't in a position to give it away. So we charge for it. The trade is that the discovery produces something real — a document you can take to any developer.

Every quote that doesn't go through discovery is a guess. Discovery is how we both end up at the right number.
