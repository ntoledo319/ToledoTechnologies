# Discovered Taste Principles

Each principle below was discovered through the Generate -> Destroy loop.
They represent this project's emergent taste DNA.

## Format

- **Date**: When discovered
- **Element**: What was being worked on
- **Principle**: The synthesized insight
- **Evidence**: The 2-3 rejection reasons that converged

---

- **Date**: 2026-04-04
- **Element**: global.css design system (taste ceiling, 9% score)
- **Principle**: A code-focused studio should look like the code it ships — precise, monospaced, sharp-edged, confident in its whitespace.
- **Evidence**: (1) Inter font signals "default Tailwind" not "code shop." (2) Rounded-2xl cards + glassmorphism are decorative, contradicting the studio's "no fluff" identity. (3) Indigo palette at hue 250 is the literal Tailwind default — zero identity.

- **Date**: 2026-04-04
- **Element**: Homepage bento grid layout
- **Principle**: When information has a clear hierarchy, the layout must reflect that hierarchy — not flatten everything into visually equal boxes.
- **Evidence**: (1) Bento grid gives conversion actions the same visual weight as informational content. (2) The grid pattern is borrowed from Apple/Vercel marketing, signaling "product platform" when this is a hands-on dev studio. (3) Sequential layout with borders-as-structure creates rhythm that matches the studio's step-by-step process.

- **Date**: 2026-04-04
- **Element**: Copy across commercial pages
- **Principle**: When your anti-marketing becomes its own form of marketing, you've replaced one kind of emptiness with another. Show specificity, don't claim it.
- **Evidence**: (1) "No fluff" appears 3+ times — it became a verbal tic, not a differentiator. (2) The nonprofit page proves real conviction exists but the commercial pages hold back. (3) FAQ answers and case study details do more selling than any hero tagline.

- **Date**: 2026-04-04
- **Element**: Frosted glass header + decorative gradients
- **Principle**: Decorative translucency signals "I used a template" to the exact audience (developers) who builds these templates. Solid, opaque, precise surfaces communicate competence.
- **Evidence**: (1) bg-white/90 backdrop-blur-md appears on ~80% of Tailwind sites post-2022. (2) The studio claims to be "no-nonsense" but the visuals are all cosmetic nonsense. (3) A solid border-bottom communicates precision; a blur communicates fashion.
