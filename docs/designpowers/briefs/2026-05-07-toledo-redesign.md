# Design Brief: Toledo Technologies Redesign & Expansion

## Problem Statement
Toledo Technologies needs to move from a "sharp," austere digital presence to an immersive, tactile "Multi-Realm Engine." The current site is direct but aggressive; the new experience must be bold and "stunning" while feeling real, welcoming, and high-performance. We need to unify disparate "design worlds" (Services, Products, Non-profit) under one "game engine" style that rewards exploration and feels like a "visual dynamic experience of a lifetime."

## Users
Our users are "burnt" professionals and skeptics who value directness and clean code but are drawn to high-craft, "one of a kind" experiences.
- **The Skeptical Lead:** Needs to see proof of quality and "directness" instantly.
- **The Creative Director:** Needs to be "wowed" by the visual engine and unique execution.
- **The High-Access User:** Expects high accessibility as part of the "art," not an afterthought.

**Ability Spectrum Focus:**
- **Reduced Motion:** Toggles or adaptive stabilization for 3D/lighting effects.
- **High Performance:** Lightweight assets and optimized code for a "real" feel on all devices.
- **Clarity:** Perfectly legible text and data even within immersive 3D environments.

## Design Direction
**The "Multi-Realm Engine."** A high-performance system that supports radical environmental shifts.
- **Radical Realms:** Huge swings in color, typography, and atmosphere between sections (Sales, Products, Non-profit). Entering a new section should feel like a "leap" into a new world.
- **Shared Style:** Unified by a "real" tactile feeling, high-craft depth, and a consistent "engine" of performance and accessibility.
- **Tactile Depth:** "Less sharp" rounded edges, soft volumetric lighting, and surfaces that react to mouse/scroll.
- **Integrated Data:** Charts and pricing are built "into" the engine's surfaces, not just laid on top.

## Constraints
- **Tech Stack:** Astro v5, Tailwind CSS v4, TypeScript.
- **Deployment:** Move from Render Free Static to Vercel Hobby for performance and optimization.
- **Legacy:** Upgrade existing content ("basic upgrade") before expanding into new realms.
- **Brand:** Must still feel like "Toledo Technologies"—bold, direct, senior-dev led.

## Existing Design System
- Current: Minimalist, monochrome, `warm-500` accents.
- Future: To be defined by the "Multi-Realm Engine."

## Success Criteria
- **Visual Impact:** "Visual dynamic experience of a lifetime"; award-winning potential.
- **Specificity:** Every element passes the "Specificity Test" (distinctive to Toledo).
- **Accessibility:** Highest standards as part of the art.
- **Performance:** "Incredible performance" alongside "incredible graphics."

## Out of Scope
- Backend development (unless required for specific dynamic data).
- Content writing for all 26+ projects (focus on core realms first).
