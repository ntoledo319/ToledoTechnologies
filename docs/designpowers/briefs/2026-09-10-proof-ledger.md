# Toledo proof pages

## Product problem
A prospective buyer needs to inspect a concrete technical sample quickly and understand its limits before authorizing work. Toledo's proof pages provide runnable demonstrations, source files and verification evidence. They do not represent client deployments or imply live merchant testing.

## People and conditions
A store owner may read on a phone while investigating an order integration. A retail software provider may inspect an offline purchasing archive. A technical reviewer needs reproducible source and negative tests. Each needs descriptive links, keyboard access, readable labels and useful content at 200% zoom. Meaning must not rely on color.

## Direction
Use the existing InsertLayout, typography, paper palette and document hierarchy. Sequential proof sections present source, test evidence and limitations. Keep the archive preview standalone and offline. No decorative images or new dependencies are needed.

A source attachment alone makes inspection less convenient. Native proof pages give each demonstration a stable address while retaining Toledo's existing site and identity.

## Success and scope
Visitors can distinguish synthetic evidence from customer work, inspect results and download curated source. Internal links and downloads work. Public files contain no credentials, customer identities or internal operating notes. The pages introduce no payment flow or pricing claim.
