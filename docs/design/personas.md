# Inclusive Personas: The Gatefold Studio

These personas represent the range of people who will interact with the "Gatefold Studio" redesign. They guide decisions on typography, interaction physics, and content structure.

---

## Jordan (The Low-Vision Professional)

**Context:** A Creative Director at a top-tier brand looking for a specialized technical partner.

**Abilities and conditions:** Low vision (macular degeneration). Relies on high contrast and significant text enlargement.

**Technology:** Large monitor, uses browser zoom (up to 300%) and occasionally a screen magnifier.

**Goals:** Assess the studio's craft and "high-fidelity" quality without straining their eyes.

**Frustrations:** Low-contrast text, small font sizes, and "hover-only" content that disappears when they zoom in.

**Environment:** Bright office with significant glare.

---

## Priya (The High-Stress Executive)

**Context:** CEO of a fast-growing startup in the middle of a technical crisis.

**Abilities and conditions:** Situational cognitive load (extreme stress). Needs to find "the fix" fast but wants to trust the partner's depth.

**Technology:** Latest iPhone, often browsing while in transit or between meetings.

**Goals:** Quickly understand if Toledo Technologies can handle their complex rescue mission.

**Frustrations:** Overly complex interactions that "get in the way" of finding information. Flashy animations that feel like a waste of time.

**Environment:** Noisy commutes, high distractions.

---

## Marcus (The Motor-Impaired Senior Dev)

**Context:** A seasoned CTO who still writes code and evaluates partners on their technical "soul."

**Abilities and conditions:** Limited fine motor control (Repetitive Strain Injury). Navigates primarily via keyboard and voice commands.

**Technology:** Specialized keyboard and voice-to-text software on a high-end workstation.

**Goals:** Verify the studio's technical depth by reading the "Technical Novels."

**Frustrations:** Sites that require precise mouse hovering to trigger interactions. "Folds" that are not keyboard accessible.

**Environment:** Quiet, ergonomic home office.

---

## Alex (The Non-Native Speaker)

**Context:** A Lead Engineer at an international nonprofit based in Berlin.

**Abilities and conditions:** English as a second language. High technical expertise but appreciates plain, narrative communication over idioms and jargon.

**Technology:** Standard laptop, fast connection.

**Goals:** Dive into the "Technical Novels" to understand the studio's engineering philosophy.

**Frustrations:** Sites that use jargon or "edgy" copy that obscures the actual service. Complex layouts that make it hard to find the "start" of a document.

**Environment:** Collaborative workspace with some ambient noise.

---

## Inclusive User Stories

### The Narrative Path

- **As Jordan**, I want to read the "Technical Novels" with high-contrast, large-scale typography so that I can evaluate the studio's quality without eye strain.
- **As Alex**, I want the content to be told as a clear narrative so that I can understand the studio's philosophy without getting lost in jargon.

### The Tactile Path

- **As Marcus**, I want the "Unfolding Gatefold" interaction to be fully triggerable via keyboard so that I can experience the site's "high-fidelity" feel without needing a mouse.
- **As Priya**, I want the unfolding to feel physically rewarding but fast, so that I can get the information I need without being slowed down by "fluff."

### The Assisted Path

- **As Marcus** using a screen reader, I want the "Unfold" action to be clearly announced so that I understand the layout has shifted to reveal new content.
