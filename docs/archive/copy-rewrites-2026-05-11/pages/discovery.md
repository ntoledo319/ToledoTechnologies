### `#sitelift-fit-check` — SiteLift Fit Check ($750 fixed)

The smallest paid front door. Productized triage for productized WordPress exits. We confirm which of the four SiteLift packages fits, which destination platform makes sense, and any data risks before quoting the full project. One to two business days.

- **For:** Small businesses on WordPress, Squarespace, or Wix wanting a fast, scope-locked migration
- **You get:** Package recommendation, platform recommendation, risk flags, fixed-price project quote
- **Inquiry:** [sitelift.toledotechnologies.com/fit-check →](https://sitelift.toledotechnologies.com/fit-check)

### `#website-audit` — Website Migration Audit ($1,500 fixed)

The deeper migration discovery. For 25+ page sites with real SEO equity, custom design ambition, or compliance needs — the kind of site where guessing the rebuild quote is malpractice. Three to five business days.

- **For:** Established sites where the rebuild quote depends on what we find inside the current build
- **You get:** Risk assessment, content inventory, redirect plan, platform recommendation, fixed-price quote for the rebuild (Exodus or Atelier)
- **Inquiry:** [web.toledotechnologies.com/audit →](https://web.toledotechnologies.com/audit)

Required first step for any internal tool, customer portal, dashboard, or custom web app. Maps the feature set, the database schema, the integrations, and the user roles. Produces a fixed-price build quote. One to two weeks.

- **For:** Operations leaders, ops-heavy businesses, founders building internal software
- **You get:** Feature map, data model, integration plan, fixed-price build quote in the right tier ($15K–$175K+)

Required first step for any production AI engagement. Maps the data sources, the autonomy level, the governance posture, the integration surface. Catches the security and cost-control problems before they're shipped. One to three weeks.

Required first step for any iOS, Android, or cross-platform engagement. Maps the screen set, the hardware features, the App Store risks, and the launch plan. The cost variance on mobile is brutal without it. One to two weeks.

### `#saas-strategy` — SaaS / Product Strategy ($7,500 – $15,000)

## Section 4: Why every project starts here

> One short section, not preachy.

> A free consultation is paid for in someone else's time, and that someone is usually the person doing the work. We don't run free consultations because the people who would do the discovery work aren't in a position to give it away. So we charge for it. The trade is that the discovery produces something real — a document you can take to any developer — and half of what you pay credits to the project if you proceed.
>
> Every quote that doesn't go through discovery is a guess. Sometimes guesses come in low and we eat the difference. Sometimes guesses come in high and you overpay. Discovery is how we both end up at the right number.

---

## Section 5: The shared rules

- **50% credit** of the discovery fee to the project deposit if proceeding within 30 days.
- **Document is yours either way.** Whether you proceed, we don't, or we route you elsewhere — the deliverable is yours.
- **Honest no.** If we don't think the project should happen, or shouldn't happen with us, the discovery says so. Productized fixed-price discoveries refund the second half if we recommend not proceeding (e.g., the Audit refunds $750 if the recommendation is "stay on WordPress with a hardening plan").
- **30-day quote validity.** The fixed-price project quote produced by discovery is valid 30 days. After that, scope is re-confirmed and the quote may shift.
- **Discovery is real work.** Done by the senior who'll do the project. Not by a sales person, not by a junior.
