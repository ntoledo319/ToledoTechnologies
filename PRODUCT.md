# Toledo Technologies commercial website

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are owners and operators of small businesses whose website
must hand work to another business system. They need to repair a known failure,
set up one defined workflow, or keep a supported workflow checked without
booking a call. Agency partners are a second audience and need the same bounded
delivery units with explicit client permission, attribution, and access rules.

## Product Purpose

The site helps a buyer identify one concrete operational problem, inspect how
Toledo proves the work, understand the scope and price boundary, and submit a
written brief. Success is an informed brief with a durable receipt, followed by
a written fit decision and an acceptance test before work starts.

## Positioning

Toledo sells deterministic, inspectable handoffs between websites and business
tools. The work is scoped around one event-to-result path and delivered with
evidence for the agreed success and failure cases.

## Operating Context

Evaluation and delivery are asynchronous and written. Buyers provide the
relevant accounts, subscriptions, permissions, and redacted examples after fit
is confirmed. Public forms never request passwords or customer data.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Workflow Repair is $1,500 for one reproducible failure in one supported
  workflow.
- Workflow Setup is $3,000 for one deterministic workflow.
- Workflow Care is $500 per month per registered workflow, with the bounded
  checks and corrective time published on its page.
- One project and three Care workflows are the initial global capacity.
- Integration Triage, file conversion, the evaluation harness, broad Care,
  discovery products, and custom work retain their existing independent terms.
- Custom tools, dashboards, reviewed document processing, quote preparation,
  and staff knowledge assistants are separately scoped and secondary to the
  workflow offers.
- No public turnaround, availability, emergency-response, or uptime promise is
  made without operational evidence.

## Brand Commitments

The incumbent open record-sleeve identity remains: warm paper, dark ink, Lora
and Outfit typography, JetBrains Mono for evidence and annotations, and a
restrained warm accent. The voice is direct, specific, and skeptical of vague
agency promises. The primary action is “Describe your workflow.”

## Evidence on Hand

The site contains studio-owned reference work, independent research, and sample
deliverables with explicit provenance and limitations. The workflow specimen is
synthetic and must always be labeled as such. It may link to the locally
generated operations artifact when that artifact is available in preview.

## Product Principles

1. State the unit of work before describing the studio.
2. Publish boundaries wherever a price appears.
3. Show inspectable evidence without implying a client result.
4. Keep inquiry and delivery asynchronous, written, and durable.
5. Preserve useful existing offers instead of forcing every problem into the
   new workflow units.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Core content and submission work in a linear mobile reading order and without
motion. Forms expose field errors, submission status, and keyboard focus.
