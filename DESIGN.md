# Commercial surface design

## World

An open record sleeve used as a working evidence packet. Warm uncoated paper,
near-black ink, fine rules, editorial serif questions, plain sans-serif body
copy, and monospaced labels make the site feel printed, handled, and auditable.
The record identity is present immediately but never blocks navigation or the
buying path.

## Composition

Commercial pages use an asymmetrical editorial grid: decisive copy on the left,
an evidence sheet or compact sleeve artifact on the right, then full-width
ruled ledgers for scope. Sections are separated by useful rules and generous
paper space, never generic card grids. Lists become registers, test records,
and written handoff sequences when the content supports that form.

## Type and color

Lora carries questions and editorial headings. Outfit carries body copy and
controls. JetBrains Mono is reserved for identifiers, test states, prices, and
measured evidence. The palette uses warm paper, carbon ink, muted oxide red,
and a small moss-green success signal.

## Interaction

One optional sleeve-reveal motion is allowed on the homepage; essential content
is visible before it runs. Links and controls receive inked underline, focus,
hover, loading, success, and error states. Reduced-motion users receive no
transform choreography.

## Surface mode

Persuade. The first viewport earns one action by naming the problem, showing a
concrete proof form, and making the written next step obvious.
